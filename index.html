📚 Financial Guide & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Learn formulas, calculation methods, and financial planning tips

🧾 How to Calculate GST Online?

Exclusive GST (Add GST): Tax is added on top of net base price. Formula: GST = Amount × Rate / 100.

Inclusive GST (Remove GST): Base price is extracted from gross price. Formula: Base = Amount / (1 + Rate / 100).

💰 How is Loan EMI Calculated?

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) formula: EMI = P × R × (1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N - 1) where P is Principal loan amount, R is monthly interest rate, and N is loan tenure in months.

📈 What is Step-Up SIP Calculator?

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows wealth compounding. Step-up SIP automatically increases your monthly investment percentage (e.g. 10% annual increase) every year, maximizing overall portfolio returns.

🏦 Fixed Deposit (FD) Compounding Rules

Fixed Deposit interest compounds quarterly in Indian banks. Formula: A = P × (1 + R / (N × 100))^(N × T) where A is maturity amount, P is principal, N is compounding frequency, and T is tenure in years.

🔄 Recurring Deposit (RD) Calculation Rules

Recurring Deposit calculates maturity by compounding each monthly installment till tenure end. Indian banks compound RD quarterly for maximum return accuracy.

💼 Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 Rules

Gratuity is paid to employees completing at least 5 years continuous service. Formula: Gratuity = (Basic + DA) × 15 × Years / 26. Income tax exemption limit is up to ₹20 Lakhs in India.
